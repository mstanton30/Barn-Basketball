Add player

iPhone: tap the Name field, then AutoFill Contact → Other Contact above the keyboard. Check the details before saving. If AutoFill is unavailable, import a shared .vcf contact card.

Phone numbers stay in your browser, not in the public GitHub source code.

Invite player

Step 1: Tap Open Messages below. Check the recipient and tap the blue Send arrow in Messages. Opening a draft alone does not send anything.
1. Open Messages Open Messages without text

If Messages doesn't open or the text is missing, copy the invitation, open Messages without text, then paste and send it manually.

Step 2: After you press Send in Messages, return here and confirm below. Barn Basketball cannot verify a text was sent.

Review no-shows

Check anyone who confirmed but did not play. Other Yes players automatically count as played three hours after game start. Corrections are allowed later.
